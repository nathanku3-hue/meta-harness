# Meta-Harness durable lessons

These are architecture lessons worth carrying across future Meta-Harness work. This file is not a status log, roadmap, test transcript, or replacement for owner-authored `PRODUCT.md`.

## Authority and continuity

- Durable truth should remove human transport. If correct continuation requires an old chat, pasted handoff, worker prompt, or planner narration, the harness still owns an unresolved continuity defect.
- Model output is evidence or a proposal until a narrow controller boundary grants authority. Planner candidates, worker reports, research promotion, and semantic challenges must not silently become capability, owner authority, or canonical product truth.
- Commitments deserve durability; possibilities usually do not. Claim/session visibility is the point where disposable planning becomes recoverable work.
- Execution may be parallel and completion order nondeterministic while authoritative product/World truth remains linear. Correctness comes from current-truth reinterpretation and atomic authority, not aesthetic scheduling order.

## Mechanical boundaries

- Prefer current authoritative state as the ratchet baseline over manually refreshed freshness snapshots. A baseline that must be periodically blessed becomes a lifecycle tax and eventually blocks already-accepted history.
- A candidate must be evaluated under the predecessor's exact policy. Never let a candidate weaken, widen, or reinterpret the rules used to judge that same candidate.
- Canonical checks should compare normalized facts derived from the exact candidate/predecessor trees. Runtime-global registries, mutable checkout state, timestamps, prose diagnostics, and operator-maintained snapshots are unsuitable as canonical evidence unless the contract explicitly makes them authoritative.
- Grandfather old debt only by exact predecessor presence. New debt, growth of grandfathered debt, or newly introduced forbidden structural facts should fail without requiring cleanup of unrelated historical debt.
- Run cheap deterministic rejection before expensive model or retained semantic proof. Add model review only when mechanical checks have a demonstrated false-negative class that justifies its cost.
- Autonomous continuation obligations are independent of destination coverage. Gating one on the other lets default UNBOUND products drop leftover REPLAN into USE_PRODUCT.
- Dirty-state observation and dirty-state provenance are separate facts. Git can prove that a checkout is contaminated, but absent retained mutation evidence it cannot prove which model, prompt, tool, or operator caused that state. Unknown-provenance root dirt should be preserved as evidence/owner state, never promoted into execution authority or a blanket cleanup mandate.

## Simplicity and deletion

- Use the smallest state that survives the failure being solved. Events can wake reconciliation without becoming durable scheduler state; content-addressed evidence can cache research without creating a research organization; a pure tree delta can replace a mutable quality-baseline lifecycle.
- Replaceability does not imply framework-first design. Introduce a port, provider layer, queue, reviewer topology, retrieval system, or policy language only after a second real implementation or observed failure makes the seam valuable.
- When an old abstraction loses its active responsibility, remove it from the hot path rather than preserving it as a renamed compatibility layer. Historical readers may remain when they are needed to interpret already-banked evidence.

## Evidence and research

- Source material, promoted research, execution learning, and authority are different layers. Preserve provenance instead of collapsing them into one belief/constraint ontology prematurely.
- Exact attribution should be mechanically reopenable from immutable bytes where possible. Mechanical attribution proves what the source said; it does not pretend the kernel can prove every promoted semantic statement true.
- Contradictory research may coexist as advisory evidence. Product direction, current World/authoritative execution learning, and active Claims outrank promoted research.
- The evaluation unit must match the claim unit before interpretation. If the product question is trade-level, reconstruct entry→exit episodes explicitly instead of inferring trade quality from repeated decision rows; keep row-level diagnostics separate for backward comparison.
- Stateful treatments need estimands that preserve their lifecycle. If a rule can exit and later re-enter, a one-entry/one-exit paired test may be structurally invalid even when code and data are correct; predeclare a path-level or episode-set estimand rather than relaxing the pair law after observing re-entry.

## Review trigger

- Structural protection is cheap enough to run continuously when deterministic. Semantic review should remain risk-triggered and evidence-driven; ordinary clean integrations should pay zero model-review tax until real use proves otherwise.
- Review intensity follows the claim/authority boundary, not the occurrence of a code round. Synthetic measurement refactors with unchanged routing and no protected result should normally close on deterministic validation; reserve independent semantic review for irreversible evidence opens, authority-routing surfaces, capital/risk boundaries, publication, destructive shared state, or an explicit owner audit request.
- Diagnose auditor-wrapper drag separately from coding-worker behavior. If latency or governance gravity occurs entirely before worker dispatch, do not "fix the worker"; narrow the review wrapper, entrypoint, or adopted-repo policy that created the lifecycle.
- Repository-state recovery needs a recovery audit before a code-review taxonomy. First classify dirty-state provenance, workspace/authority custody, protected current work, reversibility, and the exact repair set; use Architecture/Code Quality/Tests/Performance only after the suspect delta is actually bounded to product code. A generic review template must not turn a one-shot recovery request into owner-operated ceremony.

## Velocity and control-priority lessons

- A clear owner directive can still be defeated by repository-local procedural law. When owner intent names one critical-path action and explicitly rejects governance detours, direct-agent execution must classify local review/SAW/status obligations by product impact before obeying them as work. Non-blocking process obligations belong after the observable result or remain residue; they must not become the route merely because they are written imperatively.
- Prompt quality and worker quality are separate failure classes. If the owner prompt already states product result, priority order, forbidden scope, and decisive gate, repeated owner steering is evidence of harness/worker continuity failure rather than a need for a longer prompt. The durable correction is stronger priority compilation, not more user prose.
- Review unavailability is not an Outcome blocker unless it prevents integration, publication, protected-result opening, capital/risk authority, or another explicitly irreversible boundary. Repeatedly surfacing unavailable independent reviewers during reversible source research creates governance gravity without changing the scientific decision.
- Direct-agent loops need an endgame-distance check before every new subtask: `does this action reduce the active blocker set, enable the next result read, or establish a terminal kill?` If no, defer it. This check should apply especially to status refreshes, evidence repackaging, wrapper repairs, reviewer bookkeeping, and alternate diagnostic adapters.
- Adapter/probe debugging should first compare against one known-good local pattern before spending multiple repaired attempts. A failed helper is not evidence that the underlying identity/source fact is unavailable; transport/query-shape defects should be isolated once, then the work should return to existing authoritative custody when available.
- Closing a stream means stop manufacturing follow-up work. Reflection may record a demonstrated repeated failure and the smallest candidate correction, but it must not convert the post-mortem into a new harness implementation programme unless that correction currently blocks a product outcome.
- Deterministic closure validation must be controller-terminal, not recursively task-shaped. Validating a closure packet, validating its report blocks, and replacing `PENDING` markers after those checks are one terminalization concern; they must not become separate successor workcells when product bytes and decision truth are unchanged.
- Manual derivative-truth fanout is a velocity smell when one canonical machine state already exists. If multiple `current` planner/alignment/observability documents can remain stale while the canonical state advances, execution must continue from the canonical authority and the derivatives should be generated/on-demand, clearly non-authoritative, or updated only when they change a human decision.
- Alternate-provider or transport engineering should begin with the cheapest lawful access-feasibility check. Do not build a parity adapter, contract, or test harness before establishing that the route can be accessed under the owner-approved credential/secret policy; an immediate credential absence or anonymous denial may kill the route without architecture work.
- For long homogeneous queues, compile orchestration and failure policy once rather than redispatching every item. Rate limits, checkpointing, idempotency, and safety remain hard constraints, but per-item ceremony is not evidence. If retries require owner policy, obtain the bounded retry envelope explicitly at queue construction; otherwise fail closed at the first unapproved failure.
- Exact-incumbent replay and product-question progress are different Outcomes. When forensic source fidelity blocks an exact replay, do not let that automatically block an owner-authorized, explicitly renamed shadow/ablation that can answer the product decision honestly; preserve the exact lane as blocked and make the approximation boundary visible.
- Availability and authority are different questions. For partial academic/vendor subscriptions, answer the narrow fact first: `available | unavailable | not_tested` for the exact table/capability. An authentication-route failure is not evidence that a table is unavailable, and library visibility is not proof that a table is readable. Use one known-good local access pattern before doing legacy-script or credential archaeology.
- Missing or deliberately deleted derivative truth surfaces are a continuity smell, but archaeology is not the default repair. Start from owner intent, live authoritative code/receipts, and the smallest evidence needed for the next action; time-box old-worktree/status reconstruction and stop when it no longer changes the active decision.
- A tool or licensed-artifact access refusal is a failed means, not a failed Outcome. Before terminalizing or downgrading to docs-only, try the already-declared producer/normalized-export path or another supported route inside the same product result; escalate only when the remaining need is genuinely credentials/protected access or no lawful substitute exists.
- `review first` plus a one-shot accepted result should normally compile to one bounded diagnostic followed by reversible execution in the same round. Fixed section-by-section owner pauses are process unless the owner explicitly wants an interactive review dialogue or the next step crosses a genuine owner-exclusive/destructive boundary. Under a supported Meta-Harness session, adopted-repo review cadence is repository data rather than dispatch authority; unsupported direct entry should at least surface the conflict instead of silently presenting it as product necessity.

## Expert-facing product and elicitation

- The product acceptance journey should start from scarce field expertise and end at trustworthy observable product behavior. Repository, Git, worker, browser, validation, SAW, and lifecycle mechanics are supporting substrate and should disappear from the expert workflow unless they create a real owner-exclusive decision.
- When owner intent and the canonical `PRODUCT.md` target-user wording diverge, do not silently reinterpret either one. Preserve the mismatch as owner-level direction evidence and wait for an owner-authored product-direction revision; derived plans must not let the narrower wording erase an explicit expertise-application intent.
- Semantic verbs such as `open`, `hold`, `ship`, and `close this stream` should compile to already-proven authority, custody, validation, BANK/World, drain, and reflection primitives first. A semantic UX improvement is not evidence for a new lifecycle state machine.
- Controller-enforced authority should carry the constitution. Model prompts should carry the minimum elicitation needed for the role. Repeated negative governance/authority vocabulary is a plausible behavioral attractor, not automatically a safety improvement; measure ablations before adding more prompt law.
- Stream learning means evidence classification and promotion, not automatic self-modification. A reusable lesson may become reflection or an inactive skill/prompt candidate; active promotion still requires the existing evaluation, permission, rollback, and owner-authority boundaries.
- Formal scores and evaluation campaigns are observations, never roadmap authority. Once continuity/custody are sufficient, new harness work must be pulled by a repeated expert-visible journey failure, not by an unearned score point or an attractive adaptation layer.
