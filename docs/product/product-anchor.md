# Meta-Harness Product Anchor

Status: active concise product authority
Intent source: `docs/product/product-intent.md`, including the 2026-08-02 minimal-outcome amendment

## Endgame

One solo developer/researcher uses AI to ship complex, multi-module products that require engineering and specialist knowledge, with high product throughput, low human friction, reliable continuation, and minimal permanent harness machinery.

## Operating result

A fresh session can resume one product outcome, preserve the user’s current checkout, execute inside a clean isolated worktree, run a real Product Episode early, reuse unchanged evidence, make at most one smallest repair, and stop after a useful result without a long prompt or automatic successor drift.

## Normal flow

```text
PLAN -> WORK -> AUDIT -> CLOSE
```

- PLAN freezes one Product Episode and Slice Charter.
- WORK executes the charter and cannot change roadmap or acceptance.
- AUDIT evaluates only the current slice and cannot choose a successor.
- RESEARCH opens only for one named decision.
- RETROSPECT opens only after closure, route abandonment, exhausted repair budget, repeated failure, a major model change, or explicit request.
- HANDOVER is generated state, not a reasoning round.

## Proof order

```text
Product proof -> System proof -> Release proof
```

Tests, reviews, packages, hashes, custody, and scores cannot substitute for a missing real Product Episode.

## Action policy

Reversible repository work is notify-first and proceeds automatically in isolation. Existing user work is never auto-stashed, reset, cleaned, overwritten, force-pushed, or mixed into the slice.

External or irreversible effects are announced before execution and remain subject to configured deterministic controls. Notification does not imply an approval wait unless a hold is explicitly configured.

## Explicit non-goals

- no automatic successor planning;
- no universal owner-approval workflow for reversible work;
- no mandatory phase choreography;
- no broad orchestration daemon, queue, swarm, or hook framework;
- no full research survey injected into coding context;
- no mandatory retrospective after every round;
- no product progress claim from documentation, tests, reviews, packages, hashes, or scores alone;
- no backward compatibility for retired orchestration concepts.
